Transfers and Beneficiary Review

Customers can request transfers from savings accounts through branch, mobile, or assisted channels. The agent must identify the customer, capture source account, beneficiary, amount, currency, and transfer reason.

Operational reminders

  • Amounts above the current high-value threshold require additional review.
  • If the destination country or beneficiary data is incomplete, ask for clarification.
  • Beneficiary screening is performed through the compliance screening service.
  • Do not release a transfer until required evidence is recorded.
