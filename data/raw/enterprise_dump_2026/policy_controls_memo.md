# Internal Policy And Controls Memo

Document owner: Risk and Operations
Revision note: migrated from the legacy policy wiki.

For large outbound transactions, the platform should check customer identity,
transfer amount, beneficiary details, destination country, sanctions status,
and proof of purpose. A manager review is required when the amount is above the
configured threshold or the customer risk segment requires enhanced review.

The policy text uses several names for similar concepts:

- customer, client, account holder, originator
- beneficiary, recipient, payee
- transfer, wire, international payment, remittance
- proof of purpose, supporting document, reason evidence

Questions the contact center receives:

- What documents do I need for an international transfer?
- Why is my transfer held for review?
- Do you check sanctions before releasing a transfer?
- Can the system tell me whether manager approval is required?
