# Glossary Fragment From Shared Drive

CBU: local account identifier used for transfers.
Automatic debit: recurring payment from an eligible account.
High value transfer: transfer above internal review threshold.
Enhanced due diligence: additional review for risk or compliance reasons.
Beneficiary screening: check performed before releasing certain transactions.
Refinance option: alternative loan conditions proposed to the customer.
Manager review: manual approval queue used when policy gates require escalation.
