Call center notes - loan refinance.

Agents say customers usually ask to lower the monthly installment, extend the
term, compare new conditions, or understand why a refinance offer was declined.
The current manual tells agents to identify the customer, review current loan
status, calculate available conditions, compare refinance options, collect
missing documents, and submit a refinance request for approval when required.

Known phrases:
- quiero bajar la cuota
- necesito mejores condiciones
- como calculan las condiciones
- por que no califico para refinanciar
- tengo atraso, puedo refinanciar?

System names seen in old documentation:
- loan.conditions.calculate
- loan_refinance.compare
- loan_refinance.create
- approval.update
