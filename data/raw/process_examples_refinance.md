# Process examples for refinance

## Example 1

The customer requests a lower monthly payment.
The advisor checks current account state.
The process evaluates eligibility.
If eligible, the system prepares a refinance proposal.
The customer reviews and signs the offer.

## Example 2

The customer wants to combine two overdue installments.
Support verifies account history and required documents.
Collections confirms whether a special exception applies.
The workflow continues only after approval.

## Example 3

The branch agent opens the refinance case.
The customer record is validated.
The plan is presented.
The selected refinance tool computes a new schedule.

## Example 4

The contact center receives a cancellation request for automatic payment.
The system updates the recurring payment setting.
The case is logged for audit.
