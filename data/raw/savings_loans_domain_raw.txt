Savings Accounts and Loans - Domain Documentation

Overview:
This document provides a synthesized domain description for retail banking focused on savings accounts and consumer loans. It consolidates glossary terms, common processes, business rules, compliance notes, and example utterances for NLP intent creation.

Glossary (sample):
- Savings Account: A deposit account that earns interest, used for saving money.
- Term Deposit: A time-bound deposit with fixed interest rate.
- CBU: Clave Bancaria Uniforme, unique bank account identifier.
- Interest Rate: The percentage paid by a bank on deposits or charged on loans.
- APR: Annual Percentage Rate for loans, includes fees.
- Collateral: Asset pledged to secure a loan.
- Principal: The original sum borrowed or deposited.
- Installment: Periodic payment toward loan principal and interest.

Common Processes:
1. Account Opening
   - Customer identification
   - Document collection (ID, proof of address, tax ID)
   - Risk and KYC screening
   - Account creation and CBU assignment
   - Initial deposit and product enrollment

2. Savings Account Maintenance
   - Deposit and withdrawal processing
   - Interest accrual and posting schedule
   - Account statements generation
   - Dormancy and closure procedures

3. Loan Application and Origination
   - Collect application details (amount, term, purpose)
   - Verify identity and income
   - Check credit history and scoring
   - Calculate eligibility and propose terms
   - Request human underwriter approval for borderline cases
   - Disbursement and repayment schedule setup

Business Rules (examples):
- Minimum initial deposit for savings account: 100 units of currency.
- Savings interest is compounded monthly and credited on the last business day.
- Loan eligibility requires debt-to-income ratio below 40% unless supplemental collateral provided.
- Loans above 50,000 require manual underwriting approval.

Compliance Notes:
- Collect and retain KYC documents for 5 years.
- Ensure customer consent for electronic statements.

Example Utterances (savings):
- "Quiero abrir una cuenta de ahorro"
- "Cómo puedo crear una cuenta para ahorrar?"
- "Cuál es la tasa de interés para cuentas de ahorro?"
- "Necesito cerrar mi cuenta de ahorros"

Example Utterances (loans):
- "Quiero solicitar un préstamo personal"
- "Cuál es la tasa para préstamos a 12 meses?"
- "Cómo refinancio mi préstamo?"
- "Necesito pagar mi cuota de préstamo"
